Process

How it works

Four steps. Clear handoffs. You always know what’s next.

1. Intake

Fill out the short Get Started form (or email). Share what you do, who you serve, and whether you need a new site, automation, or both.

2. Scope & confirm

You’ll get a plain-language proposal: package, timeline, what’s included, and what’s not. Work starts after you confirm.

3. Build & review

Draft pages go up for your feedback. We tighten copy, CTAs, and forms until it feels right on a phone — because that’s how most customers will see it.

4. Launch & Care

Site goes live. You get a short handoff. If you choose a Care Plan, monthly edits and checks continue so the site doesn’t go stale.

What I need from you

Logo (if you have one), business hours, services list, contact preferences, and any photos you’re proud of. If you don’t have those ready, we can start with clear text and add assets later.

Typical timeline

Launch Site: about 1–2 weeks after materials are in. Online System: closer to 2–3 weeks, depending on automation complexity. Rush work only when both sides agree in writing.
