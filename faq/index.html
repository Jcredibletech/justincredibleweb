FAQ

Common questions

Short answers. If yours isn’t here, email or book a call.

How long does a Launch Site take?

Usually 1–2 weeks after I have your logo, services list, and contact details. Online System projects run closer to 2–3 weeks.

Do you write the copy?

Yes — clear, local copy based on what you tell me. You’ll review and approve before launch.

Where does the site host?

GitHub Pages is the default (fast and free for many small sites). Other static hosts work too if you already have a preference.

Can I update the site myself?

Small text changes can go through a Care Plan. If you want a CMS later, we can scope that as a separate project.

What’s the difference between Automate Ops standalone and the add-on?

Standalone ($3,500) is automation without a full new site build. The $1,500 add-on attaches to Launch Site or Online System when you’re already building with me.

Are Care Plans required?

No — but they’re strongly recommended. Sites go stale without someone watching forms, links, and small content drift.

Do you invent testimonials or client logos?

No. What you see here is what you get — real packages, real process, no fake social proof.

How do payments work?

Scoped in the proposal before work starts. Discounts, refunds, and payment exceptions go through Justin directly.

Still deciding?

Start the intake — even a rough note helps me point you at the right package.
